// Problem & Solution

Why Buddy Exists

// THE PROBLEM

Doctors and nurses are constantly occupied with multiple patients. When they're attending to one, others are left without assistance — unable to get their medication, water, or basic care. Patients suffer as a result.

01

// THE SOLUTION

The Buddy Assistant Robot supports medical staff by filling the gap. When staff are busy, Buddy steps in — delivering water, medication, and organizing files, while also improving patient morale and well-being.
